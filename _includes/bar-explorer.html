The stiffness slider runs from k = 0 (left end) to k → ∞ (right end), with the resonator mass fixed at a quarter of the bar mass per cell. Drag the frequency line on the plots, or use the slider. In the propagation plot both axes use the same scale, so the slope of the line from the origin is the phase velocity and the slope of the tangent is the group velocity, in units of the bar wave speed c. The dashed diagonal is the bare bar. At each Bragg gap, the node mode has the resonators standing still, and the sign of meff sets which side of nωB the gap opens. The bar's longitudinal motion is drawn vertically, and resonator motion is capped for display.
