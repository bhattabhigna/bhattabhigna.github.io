Drag the frequency line on the plots, or use the slider, and see whether the vibration crosses the metamaterial. The plots show Bloch dispersion of an infinite mass-in-mass chain with mass ratio 0.5. The animation solves an 8-cell chain between two semi-infinite non-dispersive media, whose far ends do not reflect. Waves can still reflect where they meet the chain, which shows up on the left. Displacement is drawn vertically, and resonator motion is capped for display.
